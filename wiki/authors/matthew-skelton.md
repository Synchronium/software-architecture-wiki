# Matthew Skelton

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/team-topologies]]

## Background

Matthew Skelton is co-founder and CEO of Conflux, a consultancy specialising in fast flow of change in software organisations. He is a consultant, trainer, and speaker with extensive experience helping organisations redesign team structures and delivery practices. He co-created the Team Topologies model with Manuel Pais and has contributed widely to the DevOps and continuous delivery communities.

## Core Positions

- Team structure and software architecture are inseparable; one cannot be designed without the other.
- Cognitive load is a first-class architectural constraint that must be explicitly bounded.
- Organisation design is technical work and must involve architects and engineers.
- The goal of organisation design is fast, safe flow of software delivery — not reporting convenience or headcount optimisation.
- Team interaction modes (collaboration, X-as-a-Service, facilitating) should be explicit and should evolve over time.

## Books

### [[sources/team-topologies]] — *Team Topologies* (2019, with Manuel Pais)

The canonical book on organising technology teams for fast flow. Co-authored with Manuel Pais. Provides a practical, adaptive model — four team types, three interaction modes — grounded in Conway's Law and cognitive load theory. Widely adopted as a vocabulary and framework for restructuring engineering organisations.
