# Betsy Beyer

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/site-reliability-engineering]]

## Background

Betsy Beyer is a Technical Writer for Site Reliability Engineering at Google in New York. She is one of the four editors of the SRE book, coordinating contributions from across Google's SRE organisation.

## Core Positions

Beyer's editorial work emphasises the cultural and documentary aspects of SRE: the importance of blameless postmortems, written runbooks, and knowledge transfer. Her perspective reflects SRE as a human discipline as much as a technical one.

## Books

### [[sources/site-reliability-engineering]] — *Site Reliability Engineering* (2016)

Co-editor of this foundational collection of essays documenting how Google runs production systems at scale. Coordinated contributions from dozens of Google SREs across all sections of the book.
