# Todd Underwood

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/reliable-machine-learning]]

## Background

Site Reliability Engineer and ML systems practitioner. Co-author of *Reliable Machine Learning* (O'Reilly, 2022). Brings deep SRE expertise to ML production operations.

## Core Positions

The SRE discipline — SLOs, error budgets, monitoring taxonomies, progressive rollouts — applies directly to ML systems with some ML-specific additions around model quality signals and training pipeline reliability.
