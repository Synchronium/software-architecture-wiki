# Andrew Harmel-Law

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/software-architecture-metrics]] (contributing author, ch. 1)

## Background

Consultant and architect; extensive experience rolling out the four key metrics (DORA metrics from *Accelerate*) in enterprise delivery transformations.

## Core Positions

The four key metrics are primarily an architectural tool — their value lies not in the numbers but in the team conversations and self-directed architectural improvements they enable. Architects should use metrics to "loosen their grip on the tiller" and move toward a facilitation role.
