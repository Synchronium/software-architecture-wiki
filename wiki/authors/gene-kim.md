# Gene Kim

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/accelerate]]

## Background

Founder and CTO of Tripwire (1999–2013); has studied high-performing technology organisations since 1999. Coauthor of *The Phoenix Project*, *The DevOps Handbook*, *The Unicorn Project*, and *The Visible Ops Handbook*. Founder of IT Revolution Press. Prominent figure in the DevOps community; helped originate the State of DevOps Report with Puppet in 2012.

## Core Positions

- The Three Ways of DevOps (flow, feedback, continuous learning and experimentation) are the foundational principles behind high-performing technology organisations
- Technical debt and poor deployment practices create a death spiral that makes future improvement ever harder
- High performers are not unicorns — any organisation can adopt the capabilities that drive performance with the right leadership and commitment
- The goal is to make software delivery a competitive weapon, not a cost centre
