# Dave Farley

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/software-architecture-metrics]] (contributing author, ch. 3)

## Background

Co-author of *Continuous Delivery* (with Jez Humble); founder of Continuous Delivery Ltd; prominent voice on CD, TDD, and software engineering discipline. Long career spanning finance, telecoms, and gaming systems.

## Core Positions

Testability is not a secondary property to be added after the fact — it is the most reliable driver of all five sustainable design attributes (modularity, cohesion, separation of concerns, abstraction/information hiding, coupling). Designing for testability produces the same structural outcomes as applying design principles directly, but with a concrete, objective gate. Continuous delivery is the architectural operationalisation of deployability; the scope of a deployment pipeline equals the scope of an independently deployable unit.
