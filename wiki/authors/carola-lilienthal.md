# Carola Lilienthal

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/software-architecture-metrics]] (contributing author, ch. 4)

## Background

Dr. Carola Lilienthal is managing director of WPS — Workplace Solutions and a software architect with over 300 architecture reviews across Java, C#, and C++ codebases. Developer of the Modularity Maturity Index (MMI). Her empirical approach to architecture quality derives principles from cognitive science (chunking, hierarchical thinking, schema theory) rather than purely theoretical design principles.

## Core Positions

Architecture quality can be measured objectively through a combination of automated metrics tools and structured review. The Modularity Maturity Index (MMI) provides a 0-10 score grounded in three cognitive principles: modularity (chunking, 45% of score), hierarchy (30%), and pattern consistency (25%). Without continuous improvement cycles, architecture erosion is inevitable — implementation debt is measurable but design/architecture debt requires architectural review and is far more expensive to address.
