# Chris Jones

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/site-reliability-engineering]]

## Background

Chris Jones is a Site Reliability Engineer at Google and one of the four editors of the SRE book. His SRE work has focused on Google's App Engine platform — running a managed serverless product at internet scale.

## Core Positions

Jones's editorial contributions emphasise the operational realities of running platform services: capacity planning, configuration management, and the lifecycle of internal tools. The platform-team perspective shaped several chapters' treatment of how SRE practices scale from a single service to fleets of services and the platforms that serve them.

## Books

### [[sources/site-reliability-engineering]] — *Site Reliability Engineering* (2016)

Co-editor; helped shape the book's coverage of capacity planning, software engineering in SRE, and the management of complex platform services.
