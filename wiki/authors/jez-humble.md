# Jez Humble

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/accelerate]]

## Background

Coauthor of *Continuous Delivery* (2010, with David Farr), *Lean Enterprise*, and *The DevOps Handbook*. Started career at a London startup in 2000; spent 2005–2015 at ThoughtWorks delivering software products and consulting as infrastructure specialist, developer, and product manager. Research VP at Google Cloud (DORA). Brings deep practical expertise in CI/CD, Lean, and DevOps to the *Accelerate* research programme alongside Forsgren's academic rigour.

## Core Positions

- Continuous delivery is not just a technical practice but a driver of organisational culture and performance
- Small batches, fast feedback loops, and automated pipelines are the mechanism by which high performance is achieved
- The goal of deployment should be a non-event: boring, routine, and reversible
- DevOps practices are not at odds with security and compliance — properly integrated they reinforce each other
