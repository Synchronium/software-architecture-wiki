# Cathy Chen

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/reliable-machine-learning]]

## Background

Engineer and ML systems practitioner. Co-author of *Reliable Machine Learning* (O'Reilly, 2022), which applies SRE principles to the full ML production lifecycle.

## Core Positions

ML systems are production systems and must be operated with the same engineering discipline as any other distributed system, plus additional vigilance for ML-specific failure modes such as silent degradation from distribution shift.
