# Manuel Pais

**Books in this wiki:** [[sources/team-topologies]]

## Background

Manuel Pais is co-author of *Team Topologies* and an independent consultant and trainer focused on team interactions, flow, and organisational design. He works with technology organisations on DevOps and continuous delivery adoption, with a particular emphasis on the human and organisational dimensions of software delivery. He is Editor-in-Chief of the *DevOps Handbook* and contributes to the broader DevOps and platform engineering communities.

## Core Positions

- Effective software delivery is fundamentally a team problem, not a tooling or process problem.
- Interaction modes between teams must be explicitly designed and periodically re-evaluated.
- Platform engineering succeeds when the platform is treated as a product with real consumers and a developer experience focus.
- Organisational sensing — detecting when team structures need to evolve — is as important as the initial design.

## Books

### [[sources/team-topologies]] — *Team Topologies* (2019, with Matthew Skelton)

The canonical book on organising technology teams for fast flow. Co-authored with Matthew Skelton. Provides a practical, adaptive model — four team types, three interaction modes — grounded in Conway's law and cognitive load theory. Widely adopted as a vocabulary and framework for restructuring engineering organisations.
